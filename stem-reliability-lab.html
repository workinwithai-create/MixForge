StemLevel vs mixSignal

What the numbers mean

Exact-sum residual compares the unscaled stem sum with the mix; it catches missing energy and hidden level changes. Gain-fitted residual removes one global level mismatch before measuring what remains. More negative is better. Correlation warns about duplicated content across stems, not proof of a semantic error.

A strong reconstruction still does not prove that “vocals” contains only vocals. Pair this audit with listening and labelled test clips.

Guardrails

Analysis is capped at the first 90 seconds and downmixed to mono. Files are decoded and analyzed in this tab; nothing is uploaded.

Offset starts, different edits, lossy encoding, clipping, or missing stems can dominate the residual. A genuinely silent instrument stem may also be correct.

Experimental quality-control tool. Thresholds are product heuristics, not research benchmarks.
