StemLevel vs mixSignal

What the numbers mean

Exact-sum residual compares the unscaled stem sum with the mix; it catches missing energy and hidden level changes. Least-squares gain fit removes one global level mismatch before measuring the remaining residual. More negative is better. Correlation warns about duplicated content across stems, not proof of a semantic error.

A strong reconstruction still does not prove that “vocals” contains only vocals. Pair this audit with listening and labelled test clips.
